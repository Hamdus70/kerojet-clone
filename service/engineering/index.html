    Description for our service

    As part of Kerojet Services, our engineering team operates with a strong focus on the operator’s perspective.

    Our team of engineers at Kerojet Services specializes in all facets of jet fuel storage and hydrant engineering. We provide comprehensive support to airports, airlines, and oil companies, guiding them through fuel facility engineering from the initial concept to the start-up phase.

    We are committed to delivering tailored solutions that meet the unique needs of our clients in the fuel industry.

    Our services include:

    Technical assessments
    Feasibility studies
    Design concepts
    Basic design
    Front-End Engineering Design (FEED)
    Design reviews
    Construction supervision
    Detailed engineering
    Flushing and commissioning
    Start-up support

    Freequently Asked Question

    This section covers the most common queries about Kerojet’s aviation fuel services, ground operations, and support offerings to help ensure a smooth experience.

    What services does Kerojet provide?
    We provide aviation fueling(Jet A-1), fuel management for local, and international airlines.
    Where are your fueling stations located?
    Kerojet Services operates across key Nigerian airports and beyond, providing comprehensive aviation fueling and ground services.

    Some Locations are Abuja, Kano, Jos, Porthacourt, Sokoto, Lagos, Kebbi, Maiduguri, Yola, Kaduna with more expansions in view.
    How can I request a fueling service?
    You can request fueling through our 24/7 operations center avoffice@www.kerojetservices.com online portal, or by contacting our local agents at the airport. Furthermore, contact details are provided on the contact page.
    What types of fuel do you provide?
    We provide Jet A-1 compliant with international aviation standards.
    Are your fueling operations certified?
    Yes, all our operations comply with IATA and JIG (Joint Inspection Group) standards.
    Do you offer credit or fuel cards?
    Yes, we offer credit terms and fuel cards for qualified operators. Contact our sales team for more information.
    Can you support emergency or last-minute fuel requests?
    Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency response services and can handle last-minute requests depending on availability.
    Who do I contact for invoice inquiries and contracts?
    For invoice related questions and contracts , please reach out to our finance department at avoffice@www.kerojetservices.com
